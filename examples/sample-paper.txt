# Sample Research Paper

## Abstract

This paper argues that policy X reduced urban crime by 20 percent after implementation.

## Empirical Strategy

We compare treated cities with untreated cities from 2010 to 2020 using a difference-in-differences design.

## Results

The main table shows a statistically significant decline in crime in treated cities after policy adoption.

## Conclusion

Policy X caused a large and durable reduction in urban crime and should be adopted nationwide.
